WHY WE OWN IT
Astera sells the connectivity silicon — signal conditioning and AI fabric — that
holds large GPU clusters together. As clusters scale, the number of these parts
per GPU rises. It is a volume-times-attach-rate business layered on top of the
same buildout the rest of the book owns, but with a different failure mode than
the compute names.
