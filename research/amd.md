AMD — Advanced Micro Devices
Book: AI Trade · Opening weight 11.0% · Horizon 12+ months

WHY WE OWN IT
The AI trade is rotating from training to inference, and inference is a
memory-bound problem rather than a raw-FLOPS problem. That shift is the single
most important structural change in this sector right now, and AMD is better
positioned in inference than it ever was in training. This is the highest-
conviction name in the book and it gets the largest weight.

CLAIMS AND VERIFICATION

  CLAIM  Q2 2026 revenue was $11.5B, up 50% year over year.
  VERIFIED  AMD investor relations, Q2 2026 results release (primary source).
  Prior-year comparison $7.685B. Confirms.

  CLAIM  Data center revenue grew 107% y/y to $6.718B, now 58% of the company.
  VERIFIED  Same primary source. This is the number that matters: the data
  center segment has crossed from a side business into the majority of AMD.

  CLAIM  Margins are expanding, not being bought with price cuts.
  VERIFIED  Non-GAAP gross margin 56% vs 43% a year ago. GAAP EPS $1.38,
  +156% y/y. Growth is falling through to profit.

  CLAIM  Q3 2026 guidance is ~$13B, +41% y/y.
  VERIFIED  Company guidance in the same release, with a stated +/- $300M band.

  CLAIM  AMD holds large-scale inference agreements with OpenAI, Meta,
  Microsoft and Anthropic.
  PARTIALLY CONFIRMED  Widely reported and consistent with company statements
  at Advancing AI 2026, but I did not locate signed-contract terms in a filing.
  Treated as supporting color, NOT as the load-bearing reason to own this.

  CLAIM  Inference TAM reaches $1.3T by 2032 (32% CAGR), about 2x training.
  UNVERIFIED  Sourced to Bloomberg Intelligence via secondary reporting. A
  forecast, not a fact. Directionally useful, not relied upon.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG
  · Data center growth decelerating below ~40% y/y for two straight quarters
  · Gross margin rolling back under 50%
  · A named hyperscaler publicly standardizing away from AMD for inference
  · Total AI capex guidance across hyperscalers being cut
