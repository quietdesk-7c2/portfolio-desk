CBRS — Cerebras Systems
Book: Moonshot · Opening weight 8.0% · Horizon 12 months

WHY WE OWN IT
This is the one name that currently fits the mandate you described: enormous
sentiment, credible high price targets, a story running well ahead of profits,
and a business early enough that the outcome is genuinely undecided. Wafer-scale
chips with embedded SRAM are architecturally well-suited to exactly the
inference workloads the market is rotating toward.

THE RISKS, STATED PLAINLY  — I am not burying these

  1. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION. The company itself discloses dependence on "a
     limited number of significant customers, including OpenAI, Group 42
     Holding Ltd, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, and
     AWS." If OpenAI reworks its commitment, the thesis breaks in one press
     release. This is the single largest risk and it is disclosed by them, not
     discovered by me.

  2. IPO'd 14 May 2026 — about three months of public history. There are no
     four public quarters to check management's guidance credibility against.
     I cannot yet grade whether they do what they say.

  3. GROSS MARGIN GUIDANCE OF 38-41% for FY26, DOWN from 47% core in Q1. The
     company is guiding its own margins lower. That is either scale-up cost or
     pricing pressure, and I do not yet know which.

  4. Priced for a great outcome after the largest IPO of 2026.

WHY IT IS 8% AND NOT MORE
Four disclosed risks, three months of public history, and margin guidance
moving the wrong way. Conviction in the thesis does not entitle it to size.
