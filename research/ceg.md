WHY WE OWN IT
The binding constraint on AI is shifting from chips to electricity. Data
centers need power that is carbon-free, always-on, and available at scale, and
in the United States that describes the existing nuclear fleet almost uniquely.
Constellation owns the largest one. This is the book's answer to the question
"what breaks first," and it is not correlated with the semiconductor names.
