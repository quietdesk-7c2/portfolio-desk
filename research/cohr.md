WHY WE OWN IT
The AI bottleneck is migrating from the chip to the link between chips. At
cluster scale, copper runs out of headroom on power and latency before it runs
out of bandwidth, which forces optics into the package. Broadcom's own framing
is the tell: "our switches and XPUs are going to be lower power with optics than
with copper." Optics justify themselves on efficiency, not just speed — which
means adoption is driven by the electricity bill, the hardest constraint there is.

Coherent makes the optoelectronic components and engineered materials this
transition runs on.
